SetSprite(vaang vaang)
vaang "Yes, I am Vaang. And I gather you have already crossed paths with my sister, given that you have returned from the Veinwood. Is she..?"
Highlight(leora -o true)
SetSprite(leora sideeye)
leora "She nearly killed me, but... she lives."
SetSprite(leora confused)
leora "She said that harming her would have harmed you as well. That the two of you are soulbound. Is this true?"
Highlight(vaang -o true)
SetSprite(vaang glance)
vaang "Yes, Viin spoke the truth."
SetSprite(vaang sad)
vaang "It is not a truth I share lightly. Most who come seeking Viin do so with blades drawn. If they knew what bound us... I feared it would either stay their hand for the wrong reasons—or worse, tempt them to use me against her."
Highlight(leora -o true)
SetSprite(leora leora)
leora "Viin was more than eager to reveal it herself, though only once she was at her limit."

SetSprite(vaang glance)
vaang "Yes... that sounds like her."
SetSprite(vaang sad)
vaang "You must think me a fool to have any love left for a sister such as mine—one who sees our bond as nothing more than leverage when all else fails."

vaang "I disapprove of course, but that has never carried much weight with her. She has always gotten what she wanted through her tricks."
Highlight(leora -o true)
SetSprite(leora confused)
leora "I do not believe it was a ploy. If what she said was true—if capturing her meant harming you—then I would rather she walk free. I will not see an innocent man suffer for a crime he did not commit."

SetSprite(vaang smile)
vaang "That is... honorable, Leora. I wish I had even a fraction of your sense of justice."
vaang "I am sorry to have been an obstacle in your path. I imagine this would have been a far simpler decision if I were not in the picture."
Highlight(leora -o true)
leora "Do not speak that way, Vaang."
leora "It is not your fault that you are bound to Viin. Nor is it your fault that she chooses to... spend her days indulging in far less honorable pursuits."
SetSprite(leora smile)
leora "Regardless of what has transpired, I am glad you are alive and well."
Highlight(vaang -o true)
vaang "Thank you... I will strive to make good use of the time you have given me. If you ever need assistance, say the word and I will rush to your aide. I owe you that much."
